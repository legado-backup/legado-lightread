排版示意 · 下方正文与图示为交互占位内容
AbstractThis preview demonstrates a reading environment for research papers. The document stays at the center, while navigation, annotation and translation remain close at hand. Open the zoom control to explore its anchored menu.

1　Introduction

Reading a paper is a conversation with its ideas. A useful reading environment gives the page room to breathe and keeps frequently used controls within reach.

The document is the primary workspace. Navigation belongs on the left; optional tools open on the right. The toolbar groups related actions so that moving through a paper feels predictable.

Page navigation and magnification are separate controls. The plus and minus buttons change the scale directly. Selecting the percentage opens a menu immediately below the zoom group.

2　Background

Continuous reading supports exploration across page boundaries. Single-page and facing-page layouts offer alternate ways to study equations, compare figures and follow a longer argument.

Query→Attention→Output
Figure 1. Illustrative attention flow.

3　Model Architecture

Complex actions are progressively disclosed. Translation and assistance are available when needed, while occasional actions such as printing and presentation live in the overflow menu.

Keyboard navigation, visible focus states and predictable panel placement help maintain attention on the content. A quiet interface is one that makes its structure clear.
